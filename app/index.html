How this board works

Every deal on this page was found and checked by the maker on 27 November 2026. Each card shows the sale price, the usual price, the discount, when the price was checked, and where it was read — a store page or a deals roundup that names the store. The verdict on each deal is the maker's honest read:

  • NEW LOW — the source calls it an all-time low, the cheapest it has ever tracked.
  • STRONG DEAL — a real cut worth acting on, 25% off or more, or a rare cut on something that almost never discounts.
  • ROUTINE SALE — a small or common cut. Still a saving, but nothing to rush for.

Where the same product is cheaper — or the same price — at another store, the card says so. No deal here is invented, and no price is from before this sale: a deal whose store page no longer shows the price should be treated as gone.
